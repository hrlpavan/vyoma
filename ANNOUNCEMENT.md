# Official Launch Announcement — Vyoma™ by HRL International

**HRL International Private Limited** (Mangaluru, Karnataka, India) announces the live release of **Vyoma™**, an Urban Air Quality Intelligence platform engineered for **PromptWars Virtual — Challenge 06**.

---

### What Vyoma™ Delivers
- **Live Day/Night Apple Weather Card**: Real-time temperature, daily High/Low, 7-hour forecast strip, and an astronomical live Moon phase renderer powered by Open-Meteo.
- **Uncluttered MapTiler Street & AQI Map**: High-resolution retina street maps with a docked Map Console bar and instant global city geocoding.
- **HRL Bio-Dose & Lung-Safe Window Engine™ (Patent Pending)**: Calculates personal daily inhaled PM2.5 bio-dose (`µg/day`) weighted by household sensitivity (Kids, Elderly, Asthma/Sensitive), commute mode, and HEPA filtration—identifying the exact safest outdoor window and exposure prevented.
- **HRL Clean-Air Family Brief™ (Patent Pending)**: One-tap copyable family air brief for instant sharing.
- **Zero-Telemetry Local-First Architecture**: Engineered with HRL Design Language and Apple Human Interface Principles.
